Your spreadsheet is the CMS.

Turn a Google Sheet into lists, cards, tables and maps on any website. One script tag. No server, no API key, no build step. Free and open source.

The code for this view

Three steps

No account, no dashboard. The sheet stays yours.

Share the sheet

Share → General access → Anyone with the link, Viewer. The first row holds the column names.

Add the script

Paste the script tag once, before </body>, or in your site builder's custom code.

Place a widget

Drop in a data-cell2-embed div, or put data-cell2-* attributes on your own design.

Works where you build

Pick your platform for copy-paste code.

Small on purpose

Private by designVisitors' browsers read the sheet straight from Google. Nothing goes through our servers, so there is nothing for us to log.
Your design, or oursReady-made embeds inherit your fonts and colours; or bind any HTML you already have, Webflow included.
Safe with untrusted cellsCell HTML is sanitised, so a sheet fed by a public form cannot inject script into your site.
One file, MITPlain JavaScript, no dependencies; Leaflet loads only on pages with a map.
