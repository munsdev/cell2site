# Roadmap

Ideas, not promises. Each stays attribute-driven and optional.

- **Pagination / "load more"**: long sheets render hundreds of nodes today; `item-limit` is only a cut-off.
- **Events format with date grouping**: schedules and tour dates are the most common use; group rows under month or day headings.
- **Gallery format**: image-first grid for portfolios and menus.
- **Per-column filter chips**: one tap beats a dropdown on phones.
- **URL-synced filter state**: shareable filtered views and a working back button.
- **Image lightbox**: enlarge gallery and card images without leaving the page.
- **CSV / JSON export button**: lets visitors take a directory with them.
- **i18n strings**: "Search…", "No matches." and friends are hard-coded English.
- **Google Forms "submit a row" pairing**: a documented form-to-sheet-to-page loop for job boards and sign-ups.
- **Geocoding by address**: removes the need for Latitude/Longitude columns; needs a third-party API, so strictly opt-in.
- **Custom Leaflet marker images**: logos and pins instead of emoji.
- **Dataset size warning**: a console hint when a sheet is large enough to hurt, pointing to pagination.
